The user approved these presentation changes on 2026-10-20. They do not alter the 193 formal source records, the 100 selected formal paper IDs, the original citations, author-role review, or the patent data.

Search reports 101 entries rather than implying 101 formally published papers. The old `#conference-program` anchor still points to MBA.
- The visible section heading and index label are now `Books`. The original `#books-and-book-chapters` anchor remains valid, and chapter citations continue to identify their parent books.
- The patent cataloging paragraph and the redundant application-counting paragraph were removed. The `Chinese patents`, `US patents` and `Published US applications` headings and all citations remain.
- The shared footer no longer lists the website implementation tools. License and attribution text remain in the repository. Public biography, research content, software features and setup instructions remain because they serve visitors.

The visitor-facing copy requirement is maintained in the repository's `AGENTS.md`: maintenance, verification, cataloging and publishing explanations belong in repository documents, not in public page prose.
